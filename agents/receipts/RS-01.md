## Things that went wrong (and what they taught)

1. `peak_mem.py` first printed **0.0 MiB**: `PROCESS_MEMORY_COUNTERS.cb` was declared as `SIZE_T` instead of
   `DWORD`, so `GetProcessMemoryInfo` failed with `ERROR_BAD_LENGTH` and the return value was ignored. Fixed
   by the correct layout plus `restype`/`argtypes` on `GetCurrentProcess`, and by raising on failure instead
   of printing a zero. The numbers in this receipt are from the fixed probe (`mem_probe.py`).
2. The first preprocessing A/B compared masks on the raw 1024x1024 grid, which made the padding geometry
   look like a 0.625 IoU disagreement about the subject. Re-running it with every mask resampled back onto
   the source grid showed the real answer: the two contracts agree to IoU 0.996–0.999. The lesson is in
   `ab_norm.py`'s docstring — separate the axes before drawing a conclusion.
